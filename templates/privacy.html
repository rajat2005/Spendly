Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect information you provide directly to us. This includes your email address when you create an account, and any expense data you choose to log — such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect sensitive financial information like credit card numbers or bank account details.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide solely for the purpose of delivering and improving the Spendly service. This includes displaying your expense history, generating summaries and insights, and helping you understand your spending patterns. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your expense data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. You can delete your data at any time by deleting your account.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, or other operational purposes. These services may have access to your data only as necessary to perform their functions and are obligated to maintain confidentiality. We do not share your expense data with third parties for their own use.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We use minimal cookies to maintain your session and improve your experience. These cookies are essential for the functioning of the service and do not track your activity across other websites.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us. We are committed to addressing your concerns and ensuring your privacy is protected.
